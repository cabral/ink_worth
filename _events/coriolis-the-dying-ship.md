A freighter thought lost for a decade has reappeared on the edge of the system,
running dark and answering no hails. Whoever boards it first owns what's inside.
The Icons have smiled on you. Or someone wants you to think so.

*The Dying Ship* is a tense sci-fi one-shot in the Third Horizon. Claustrophobic,
atmospheric, and heavy on choices with consequences. Best for players who enjoy a
slow build and a creeping sense that the ship is not as empty as it looks.

Character archetypes are pre-built; pick one at the table.
