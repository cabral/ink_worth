The autumn campaign begins here. A crumbling keep on the edge of the borderlands
has started drawing treasure-seekers, and none have come back. The local reeve
will pay well for answers, and even better for whatever lies beneath.

*Secret of the Dragon Emperor* is the first session of our autumn Dragonbane
campaign, but it stands on its own as a lively dungeon romp. Newcomers welcome:
Dragonbane is fast, funny, and easy to pick up in an evening.

Bring yourself; we'll build characters together at the start.
