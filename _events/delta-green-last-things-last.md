An Agent's former handler has died, and the cleanup falls to you. It should be
simple: empty a storage unit, burn what needs burning, walk away. It won't be.

*Last Things Last* is an introductory Delta Green scenario for new and returning
players alike, with no prior knowledge of the setting required. Expect slow dread
rather than gunfire, and bring a character who has something left to lose.

Pre-generated Agents are provided. Rules are taught at the table.
